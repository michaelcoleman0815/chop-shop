# Changelog

What changed in each version of Cutalong, newest first. The app shows this in Settings, under Changelog, and GitHub's release notes use each version's section too.

Changes waiting for the next version go under "Unreleased". When the version goes up, rename that heading to the new version and the day it ships.

## Unreleased

## 0.5.0 — 2026-11-25

- Claude looks at frames of your footage before it plans an edit, as well as reading the words.
- Every edit says what it should cost before it starts, and the most it may come to. Anything over 10 cents waits for your OK, and nothing more is started that would go past the most it said. A change soon after an edit costs a few cents. Settings, under AI, has "Tell me the cost first".
- "How hard I work on each edit", in Settings under AI. At Careful and Best, a critic who has not seen the plan scores every graphic and camera move, and what is not good enough is redone. At Best, one graphic is designed for that video alone. Both cost more than Quick, and the price says how much.
- New graphics beside you, along the foot of the frame and across it: figures, lists, quotes, money going from one company to another, timelines and headlines.
- Paper boards that explain an idea, tier lists, a title set behind you, and brand marks in 3D.
- Graphics settle in softly and leave by moving rather than only fading, and one is gone before the next comes in.
- On a Mac, the shot glides over to make room for a graphic and glides back, rather than cutting to a new zoom, and graphics keep clear of your face.
- Each video gets colours and type of its own, unlike your last few.
- A video that was edited before you brought it in keeps its own cuts and its own timing.
- A wide video starts without captions, and they sit small along the bottom when you turn them on. Captions step out of the way of a keyword, and there is a new look for them, Pop (/pop).
- On a Mac, Cutalong reads the lines on a screen recording, and a camera move over one leaves your window where it is.
- Sound effects of your own, in a Sound Effects, Sounds or SFX folder in Music, are used before the made ones.

## 0.4.3 — 2026-11-23

- Every paid call to Claude is now written down against the video it was for, so Cutalong can say what your videos really cost. Settings, under AI, shows the average for a Short and for a long-form video, and the editor's script says what the open video has cost so far.

## 0.4.2 — 2026-11-23

- Settings has one AI section instead of two. Pick Claude or Local AI from the "Edits with" menu, and only what that one needs shows below it.
- Settings has a Changelog that says what changed in each version.
- The first time you open a new version, Settings opens on the Changelog and shows what changed since the version you last opened, however many updates ago that was.

## 0.4.1 — 2026-11-23

- The Windows app is back. 0.4.0's Windows build stopped at its own startup check, so this version ships it.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-11-23

- Layer tracks: clips over and under your edit. They can be video, pictures, words, a solid colour or sound. Each sits on a word or at a time, can have keyframes on any setting, and can be trimmed by hand or by asking.
- Blends that act on your own picture, adjustment clips, and expressions on any keyed setting.
- A Mix tab, with a fader, pan, mute, solo and effects for every sound.
- Particles: snow, sparks, confetti and five more.
- Colour scopes over the preview.
- Masks you draw, mattes cut to the person or an object, and a green-screen key.
- Following an object as it moves and as it changes size.
- 3D: cards, titles with depth, shapes, lights and a moving camera. They look the same in the preview and the export.
- Claude can use all of these when you ask.
- Who is talking is found from their voices, on your computer.
- Podcast clips follow whoever is talking, and an episode can open cold.
- Podcast Shorts are cut the way the most-watched shows cut them, with no flashes at the cuts.
- A quiet sermon is turned up 24 dB at most, and the room is taken down with it.
- A quieter look: no characters, and What I've learned in place of the crew.

## 0.3.9 — 2026-11-22

- Sign in once, and your keys, settings, brands and presets are on every copy of Cutalong.
- Start opens on one question, "What are we editing today?", and the composer.
- Sign-in codes are eight digits.

## 0.3.8 — 2026-11-22

- A clip's first word stays whole.
- A cut after someone starts a line again lands in a pause.

## 0.3.7 — 2026-11-22

- Lines a preacher breaks off and begins again are cut.
- Sermon clips have six more looks.

## 0.3.6 — 2026-11-21

- The editor's chat works again and holds a conversation.
- Glitch, RGB-split and VHS transitions, smooth slow motion, steadying, and graphics pinned to what you hold up.
- Sermon clips read as one piece.
- On a phone, the text box takes the keyboard again when it comes up and goes straight down.

## 0.3.5 — 2026-11-21

- Each clip opens on its line, not on a filler or the run-up before it.
- Captions stay up until the next word, so they never blink off between words.
- Sermon clips have one-word captions in heavy capitals at the middle of the frame.
- A set of sermon clips gets six looks as unlike each other as the churches' own reels.
- A sermon's hook card and title are centred.

## 0.3.4 — 2026-11-21

- Sermon clips are cut to the level of the churches people follow.
- On a phone, listening for ums has a time limit, so it never holds up the transcript.

## 0.3.3 — 2026-11-21

- Cutalong for Windows, beside the Mac.
- Jump cuts are hidden with a punch-in, wide and close in turn.
- Each graphics font shows in its own face on the Brand page.

## 0.3.2 — 2026-11-21

- Every program inside the app keeps working after the app updates itself.

## 0.3.0 — 2026-11-21

- A new look, on set: the welcome, the stage, preset cards and a setup card.
- Choose the AI that edits with you. Settings is regrouped.
- The ums Whisper leaves out are heard.
- You can edit a Premiere sequence by chat and send it back with its cuts untouched.
- Updates download by themselves and say when one is ready.
- B-roll is offered in the chat, and the editor swaps it.
- Several players' views of one gaming session can be cut together.
- Shorts are aimed at your channel's usual length.
- The phone editor has the word track.
- Nothing typed in reply makes, writes or changes anything until its Yes is tapped.

Questions

Does it keep it under a minute?

It tells you. If the cut runs over 60 seconds, it says by how much and asks what to drop, or picks for you if you ask.

Can I make several Shorts from one video?

Yes, with /clipping. It finds the moments that stand on their own and makes each a vertical, captioned clip.

Can I change the captions?

Yes. Pick Subtitles, Hierarchy or Boxed, or set your channel’s own: how many words at a time, where they sit and their colours.

Does it upload to YouTube?

No. It exports the Short with its captions, a description, three titles and tags beside it, ready for you to upload.

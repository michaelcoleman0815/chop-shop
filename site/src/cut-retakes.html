How this was measured

An 85‑second test recording made for Cutalong’s own checks, with retakes, broken sentences and a groan planted in it. /takes cut it, and the cut was scored word by word against what was planted. One recording, not an average.

Questions

How does it pick the best take?

By rule, not by guess. It keeps the last complete take, unless an earlier one ran clearly smoother, with fewer ums, restarts and stumbles.

What if it isn’t sure?

It asks instead of cutting. Say “cut it” and it goes.

Will it cut a line I say twice on purpose?

No. Repeats you mean, like “again and again” or “very very”, are left alone.

Can I bring a take back?

Yes. Struck takes stay in the script, crossed out. Tap one to bring it back, as a new version, so nothing is lost.

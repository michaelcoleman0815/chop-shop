A day of clips, told as a story

Drop the day and say /vlog. I put the clips in the order you shot them, leave out the shaky and dark ones, and tell the day as a story.

How it tells a day

A tease, the day in order, a close

It opens on a tease of the best moment, tells the day by the clock, and closes on your sign‑off or a quiet last shot.

  1. TeaseThe laughter at 13:40
  2. 8:40The café
  3. 12:15Up the ridge
  4. 19:30The estuary at dusk
  5. CloseYou, saying it was the best day

Questions

How does it know the order?

From the time each clip was recorded. A second camera’s clips are lined up to the first one’s clock.

What does it leave out?

Clips too dark, shaky or blurred to use, and the same shot taken twice, where it keeps the better one.

Can I set the length?

Yes. Say /vlog 8 min.

Does Claude see my footage?

Three small stills from each moment, unless you turn off “See frames from your footage” in Settings. Never the footage itself.

Questions

How many clips does it make?

About one for every few minutes of the recording. Say a number, like /clipping 10, for that many at most.

How long is each clip?

30 to 60 seconds from a talk or a sermon, 15 to 60 from a stream, and 30 to 90 from a podcast.

Does it follow the speaker?

When your Mac finds a face, the vertical clip follows it. Otherwise it keeps the middle of the picture.

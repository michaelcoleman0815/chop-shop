Questions

Which words does it take out?

Um, uh, er, erm, ah, hmm and mm. Never “like”, “actually” or “literally”, which are often part of what you mean.

What about the ums the transcript misses?

Transcripts often leave them out. When the words are heard on your Mac, Cutalong listens again where no word was written, and the ums it finds go too.

Does it work on a podcast or a sermon?

Yes, more gently. A podcast keeps pauses up to 0.6 s and a sermon up to 1.5 s, and both keep the ums.
